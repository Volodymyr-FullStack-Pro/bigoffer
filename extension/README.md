# BigOffer browser extension

Two buttons. **Save this job**: one click opens the board with that link, the board reads the
page (DOU, Djinni and robota.ua fully; any other site by its title and description) and you confirm the card.

Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc): open `chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, choose "Load unpacked" and pick
this folder — or unzip `apps/web/public/bigoffer-extension.zip` and load that.

**Fill this form**: fills the empty fields of an application form with your name and contacts from the BigOffer
profile and your saved answers (salary, start date, English, format, why looking). Nothing is sent anywhere but BigOffer,
and nothing is submitted — check the fields and send the form yourself.

Permissions: the active tab (only when you press a button) and the BigOffer site. No logins or tokens — it uses your
BigOffer session in the browser.
